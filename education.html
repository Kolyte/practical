Educational benefits from computing

Education can be done anywhere using anything, but with the help of computing, it becomes far more accessible for people around the world.

Explore why

Three beneficial implementations

From sharing university resources in classrooms to international communication.

Open courseware

Universities like MIT are seen as very prestigious and difficult to enter, so it a blessing that due to technology, they are capable of utilising OpenCourseware to share course lessons created by them online for free so that others may access them. This allows for their valued education to be that much more accessible to people online.

This can be seen in action at OpenCourseWare with various courses to explore.

Adaptive learning

Wind and solar output changes with the weather, so grids need software to balance supply and demand minute by minute. Forecasting, smart meters and battery scheduling all help renewable power replace fossil fuels without blackouts.

The Carbon Intensity API shows how green Great Britain's electricity is right now.

Education for all

Satellites photograph the whole planet every few days. Image analysis can spot illegal logging, track ice loss and measure crop health far faster than people on the ground could.

Education for all turns this data into a free interactive map.

Why it matters

Education is vital for the growth of humanity and allows for leaps in development and advancements in society as a whole, being able to educate easier makes improvements far more achievable for people around the world

However, over reliance on technology can also stunt growth so it is important to not only use technology to assist with education, but to educate students on how to use the technology to help thewm as well.
