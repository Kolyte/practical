Benefits of Machine Learning in Healthcare

Cancer Detection
Medical Imaging Analysis: Deep learning models (such as Convolutional Neural Networks and Vision Transformers) review X-rays, mammograms, CT scans, and MRIs to spot tiny tumors or tissue patterns that the human eye might miss. For example, AI tools assist pathologists with prostate biopsy images and help radiologists process mammograms faster.
